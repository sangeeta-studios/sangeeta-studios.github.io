Last updated: September 2026

1. Information Collection and Use

The CG Tax Calculator app processes your financial input (such as purchase dates, sale values, and asset types) locally on your device to compute capital gains tax. I do not collect, store, or transmit your financial data to any external servers. Your financial inputs remain strictly on your device.

To improve the Service, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase. This third-party service collects anonymous, aggregated usage data (such as app opens, screens viewed, and crash reports) to help me understand how the app is used and to fix bugs.

For more information on what data Google collects, please visit the Google Privacy & Terms web page.

The app offers premium features via in-app subscriptions managed by RevenueCat and the Google Play Store. These services may collect basic transaction history to validate your purchase. I do not have access to your credit card information.
